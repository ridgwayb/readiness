Why

Each signal is compared with your own trailing baseline — every value in the series except the most recent. HRV is log-transformed because its distribution is skewed. z-scores are capped at ±2.5 so one strange night can't dominate.

Respiratory rate and wrist temperature count only when raised; running cooler or breathing slower than usual is not a penalty. Sleep is scored against a healthy range of 7–9 hours rather than more-is-better — falling short counts fully, sleeping well over counts at half weight.

Colour follows the effect on readiness, not the direction of the number. Resting heart rate rising and HRV falling both show red, because both drag the score down. The line under each name says what the metric itself did.

The weights are heuristic, not validated. Missing signals are dropped and the rest renormalised, rather than scored as neutral. Trust the band, not the decimal.
